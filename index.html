The Chain
Built for when elliptic curves fall

The facts the builder works from — re-verified against real sources before any claim ships.

Signatures

ML-DSA from block one

Transactions are verified with ML-DSA-65 and ML-DSA-87, the post-quantum standard NIST finalized in 2024. No future migration, no address moves — quantum resistance is the foundation, not a retrofit.

Consensus

Proof-of-work

Secured by open mining — 100% of every block reward goes to the miner, nothing minted to a treasury. A 27% genesis allocation (5.67M QTC) vests to early contributors and the treasury, mostly locked for year one; the remaining 73% is mined into existence.

Supply

21M QTC cap

The native coin $QTC is capped at 21 million. Fixed, scarce, and distributed through work.

Engineering

7 KB txs, aggregated

ML-DSA signatures are big — about 7 KB per transaction vs ~100 bytes for Bitcoin. Quantus answers with native transaction aggregation, posting succinct proofs on-chain and getting more efficient as volume grows.

Security

Audited in the open

Reviewed by Neodyme, Eiger, Hashcloak, and V12, with a public Immunefi audit competition before launch.

Markets

First venue: NEAR Intents

Quantus named NEAR Intents as the first exchange venue for $QTC. No listing date confirmed yet — this page will say so when there is one.
