How the numbers are made

  • Block height, accounts, transfers: from the public Quantus Subsquid indexer at sqm.quantus.com (same backend as the official explorer), delivered via a same-origin snapshot when browser CORS blocks a direct call.
  • TPS (24h): today's tx_count from daily_chain_stats ÷ 86,400.
  • Observed block time: mean gap between the timestamps of the latest 12 blocks.
  • Circulating supply (est.): 21M − (21M − 5.67M) · e−blocks/50,000,000 — the emission formula verified against docs/reference/tokenomics.md (12s blocks, 27% genesis mint of 5.67M QTC). Marked "est." because vesting unlocks move inside the genesis share.
  • 1 QTC = 1012 planck. Ages are computed from the block timestamps your clock reads.

Data: public Quantus indexer · upstream reference: Quantus-Network
