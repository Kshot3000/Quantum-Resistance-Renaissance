    Deadlines, refunds & safety rules

    ⏳ Deposit deadlines

    A live quote reserves its deposit address until deadline — the wallet requests now + 20 minutes and refuses any quote whose deadline is under 5 minutes away. Slow chains get more room: 2 hours from BTC, LTC, DOGE, BCH, DASH and ZEC.

    ↩️ Refund paths

    Deposit less than quoted: refunded to refundTo after the deadline, minus a refundFee (0.30 USDC on ETH in the live probes). Deposit more: the swap still executes and the excess goes to refundTo. No fill or price past slippage: REFUNDED.

    🔏 Tamper-proof quotes

    Every quote carries an Ed25519 signature from the 1Click manager key. The wallet verifies it, checks the echoed request repeats your amount, assets, addresses, slippage and deadline, and rejects anything that doesn't match — a SwapQuoteIntegrityException, not a silent accept.

    📬 Addresses are validated early

    1Click validates refundTo against the origin chain at quote time, so a bad refund address never reaches the deposit step. Swapping out of QTC, the wallet itself signs the deposit transfer — only transparent accounts whose key lives in the app can swap; encrypted, Keystone and watch-only accounts see the button disabled.

    🗂 Orders aren't persisted (yet)

    Close the progress screen and the app forgets the order — a swap-in's deposit address lives on in your other wallet, a swap-out's deposit shows as an ordinary transfer in activity, and 1Click keeps processing regardless. Save the deposit address yourself if you navigate away.

    🧪 No testnet for Intents

    There is no NEAR Intents testnet — the wallet team's docs were verified against the live API. Until QTC is listed, every quote attempt fails with tokenIn/tokenOut is not valid, and the swap UI stays behind the enableSwap remote flag.
