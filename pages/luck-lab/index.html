Mining is a lottery with known odds.

Every mining calculator shows you the average. The Luck Lab shows you the distribution — how long you'll actually wait for a solo block, what your month of pool payouts really looks like, and what happens when the network doubles overnight. All math is the memoryless Poisson model; all chain numbers come from the live Quantus indexer (snapshot fallback when it won't talk to your browser).

Nothing here is financial advice. Luck has no memory: a long drought never makes a block "due".

Solo luck — how long until your block?

Your miner finds blocks as a memoryless Poisson process at rate h / D per second. The wait is exponential with mean D / h — the median wait is only 69% of the mean, and 1 in 10 miners wait more than 2.3× the average.

Memoryless means merciless. After waiting the full expected time with no block, your chance of finding one in the next hour is exactly the same as it was at the start. Past droughts never "use up" bad luck — and past hot streaks never spend good luck either.

Pool variance — the smoothing machine

A fair pool pays you your proportional share of every block it finds, minus fees. The expected income equals solo mining minus the fee — but the variance collapses: instead of rare jackpots you get a steady drip. The simulator below runs real day-by-day draws.

Difficulty-shock scenarios

What if the network hashrate jumps today? Difficulty retargets every block (max +1/2048 up, −99/2048 down), so it chases the new reality — fast on the way down, slow on the way up. Your solo expected wait is recomputed at the new steady state.

Catch-up assumes the full per-block step is used every block (worst case for miners on the way up). Your expected wait depends only on difficulty and your own hashrate — the network's size matters through difficulty, not directly.

Method, formulas & sources

The math

  • Solo block finds: Poisson process, rate λ = h/D per second.
  • Expected wait E = D/h; luck quantile tp = −E·ln(1−p).
  • P(≥1 block in t) = 1 − e−t/E; P(wait > k·E) = e−k.
  • Your blocks/day ~ Poisson(h·86400/D); pool blocks/day ~ Poisson(blocks/day).
  • Retarget catch-up: ×r up → ⌈2048·ln r⌉ blocks; ×r down → ⌈ln r / ln(1−99/2048)⌉ blocks.
  • Block reward: exact on-chain R = (21,000,000 − total issuance) / 50,000,000.

Honest caveats

  • Difficulty, network hashrate, and reward are snapshots — they move every block.
  • No orphan/stale rate, no pool downtime, no fee tiers beyond the one you set.
  • QTC has no liquid market price used here — figures are in QTC, not dollars.
  • Simulations are seeded and reproducible, not predictions.
