Data sources

  • Live: the public Quantus Subsquid indexer (sqm.quantus.com/v1/graphql) — per-address account_by_pk balances plus chain head. The indexer allowlists only official Quantus domains for browser CORS, so community Pages apps fall back to snapshots when the browser can't reach it.
  • Snapshots (same-origin, refreshed server-side): data/whales.json (top-200 balances), data/vesting.json (all 48 vesting schedules), data/flows.json (transfers ≥ 1 QTC in the recent window + largest all-time), data/miners.json (coinbase attribution), data/live.json (chain head).
  • On-chain semantics: address format SS58 prefix 189; human checkphrases from Quantus-Network/qp-human-checkphrase; vesting math from Quantus-Network/chain pallets/vesting + mainnet_vesting.rs.
