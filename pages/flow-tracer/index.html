ⓘ Methodology & honest limits

  • Source. The public Subsquid indexer at sqm.quantus.com/v1/graphql (transfer entity: amount, from/to, block height, timestamp, fee, extrinsic). The browser build tries it live first; GitHub Pages is not on the indexer's CORS allowlist, so the app falls back to the same-origin snapshot.
  • Snapshot contents. Every transfer ≥ 1 QTC in the 15,000 blocks before capture, plus the 600 largest transfers of all time, plus the full 22-transfer block-1 genesis allocation (merged from the supply-audit snapshot, same indexer). Miner-reward dust (< 1 QTC) is excluded by design.
  • Snapshot format. Stored columnar (format v2): addresses and fees are dictionary-encoded and each transfer is one compact row — lossless (every field, including fee and extrinsic id, survives decoding exactly) at roughly half the download of a plain object list. Decoded in-page by the shared assets/flows-decode.js.
  • What tracing misses. Transfers below 1 QTC, transfers outside the window, and any move after the snapshot was captured. A trace that ends is a trace that ran out of dataset, not necessarily out of chain.
  • Patterns are shapes. Peel chains, fan-outs and fan-ins are structural heuristics. They do not identify owners and do not allege anything about intent.
  • Refresh. node scripts/fetch-flow-data.mjs regenerates data/flows.json; commit it to refresh the snapshot.
