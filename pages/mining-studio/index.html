    📊 Benchmark Interpreter

    Run ./quantus-miner benchmark --gpu-devices 1 --duration 30, then punch your number in. We grade it against the verified reference for your card class.

    • CUDA vs WGSL: upstream's own Clore benches show CUDA ~4.2× faster than the wgpu path on the same host — NVIDIA users should prefer the --cuda-gpu build.
    • Throttle: mining while you work? Add --gpu-throttle-ms 50 to give the desktop breathing room.
    • Keep-alive: the node drops idle miner connections after 60 s — the bundled miner heartbeats every 5 s; third-party miners must too.
    • CPU is for the node, not the money: ~15 MH/s per CPU thread vs 1+ GH/s on a modern GPU. A 16-core CPU adds roughly 2% to a single RTX 4090 rig.

    🌀 The Wormhole System

    The single most important thing to understand before you mine: rewards are never sent to an address you paste in. They go to a privacy-preserving wormhole address derived from your preimage — automatically.

    1

    Your preimage

    A 32-byte secret (inner_hash) generated on your machine by quantus-node key quantus --scheme wormhole. Never shared.

    →
    2

    Poseidon hash

    The node hashes your preimage with Poseidon — the same ZK-friendly hash behind QPoW. One way, quantum-resistant.

    →
    3

    Wormhole address

    Your reward address. The node derives it at startup and logs it. Rewards land here with no claim step — private by default.

    Save the inner_hash. It is effectively your mining private key. Lose it and the rewards are unspendable. Back it up offline, encrypted.
    Check the logs. On startup the node prints "Mining rewards will be sent to wormhole address …" — confirm it matches the Address from key generation.
    Exiting costs 0.04%. Moving funds out of a wormhole address pays a volume fee (4 bps) — half burned, the rest to the miner of the exit block. High-security reversible transfers cost 1%, fully burned.
    Can't find your rewards? You're probably looking at the wrong place: rewards go to the wormhole address, not the preimage string. Query the address on the Block Explorer.

      📡 Monitoring

      A miner who isn't watching isn't mining. Wire these up on day one.

      Node logs

      Watch for Imported #… — if block numbers stop climbing, you're stalled.

      tail -f ~/.local/share/quantus-node/chains/mainnet/network/quantus-node.log

      Prometheus

      Node metrics at http://localhost:9615/metrics (override with --prometheus-port). Upstream docs disagree on the default — 9615 in the current mining guide and sc_cli default, 9616 in the older chain/MINING.md table — confirm with quantus-node --help.

      Miner metrics

      The external miner exposes http://localhost:9900/metrics (override with --metrics-port).

      Telemetry

      telemetry.quantus.cat — find your --name to confirm the network sees you.

      Local RPC

      Latest block, straight from your node:

      curl -s -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"chain_getBlock","params":[]}' http://localhost:9944

      Reward watch

      Query your wormhole address on the QTC Block Explorer — rewards need no claim step.
