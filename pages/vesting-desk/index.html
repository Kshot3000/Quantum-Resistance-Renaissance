⚙ Vesting 101 — the exact rules

The unlock formula

From pallets/vesting/src/lib.rs (vested_amount), times in milliseconds since the unix epoch:

  • Before cliff: 0 claimable.
  • At or after end: the full total.
  • Between: floor(total × (now − start) / (end − start)) — linear, rounded down, measured from start.

How to claim

Vesting.claim(schedule_id) — pallet index 22, call index 0. Claim is permissionless: any signed account can call it for any schedule, and the payout always goes to the stored beneficiary. This is the only claim path for beneficiaries that cannot sign (wormhole addresses, high-security accounts) — a third-party "ping" is all it takes.

Payout quirks

  • 25-QTC chunks: non-final claims pay down to multiples of 2,500 × the 0.01-QTC payout quantum. The leftover rides to a later claim; the final claim pays the exact remainder.
  • One claim per day per schedule (MinClaimInterval = MILLIS_PER_DAY).
  • Pull-based pot: the unclaimed allocation sits in the pallet's sovereign pot; no locks or freezes ever touch a beneficiary account — any address, even keyless, can be a beneficiary.
  • Admin powers (treasury origin): create schedules, end early (vested part to beneficiary, rest back to treasury), retarget a beneficiary (lost-key remedy — everything unclaimed follows the schedule to the new wallet).

Genesis audit

The 27% TGE mint is 5,670,000 QTC (mainnet_vesting.rs, compile-time asserted): spreadsheet grants 4,957,502 + intents grant 42,000 + treasury liquidity 210,000 + treasury vesting remainder + 3-QTC seed endowments for each treasurer and tech-collective member. The indexer shows 5,669,940 QTC across 48 schedules — the rest is seeds and the pot's existential-deposit buffer.

⚑ Methodology & honesty

  • Live data is attempted from the public Subsquid indexer first; GitHub Pages is not on the indexer's CORS allowlist, so the app falls back to the baked data/vesting.json snapshot — the badge above always says which one you are seeing.
  • "Claimable" is an estimate: the chain evaluates vesting at the claim block's own timestamp, and only one claim per schedule per day is accepted. Treat the estimate as a lower bound, not a promise.
  • Unlock projections assume the pallet math stays as audited; an admin end_schedule or retarget_schedule can change any schedule. The desk never moves funds and never asks for keys.
  • All QTC math is BigInt plancks (1 QTC = 1012 plancks) — no floating point anywhere.
